Research

Tropical cyclone intensity estimation in the North Indian Ocean

In the Arabian Sea and Bay of Bengal no aircraft flies into the storm, so satellite imagery is often the only view of its core. This 2.1M-parameter CNN-LSTM with cross-attention reads 12 hours of infrared frames plus storm position and motion to predict wind speed. Pre-training on 14,302 frames from 264 Atlantic and Pacific storms, then fine-tuning locally, fixed the model's blind spot on severe cyclones.
